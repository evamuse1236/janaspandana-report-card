0: Where does a complaint go?

1: You file. You get a Grievance ID. An officer works on it, then closes it and uploads an Action Taken Report.

2: If you say you are not satisfied, it reopens and climbs a level. Seven days per level. You can do this twice.

3: After the third level, the app has nothing left for you.

4: From here, you leave the app: RTI, the Lokayukta, or Sakala.

0: Seven fixes, and where on the journey they land.

1: Most fixes land at the very first step: search that works, fewer lists, voice, plain words.

2: While it is in progress: show the days left and the officer's name.

3: At closure: always allow reopen, with the report as proof. And where the app hits a dead end, show the error and a helpline.

4: Do this, and 'resolved' will finally mean the light is on.

Questions we expect

What is the official time limit?
21 days for grievances, 7 per level, since 23 May 2025. The CM’s “15 days” was a verbal order for one 2023 event. portal, 22 Oct 2026; Deccan Herald, 28 Nov 2023
Does “disposed” mean “fixed”?
No. The website says “disposed”, the dashboard says “resolved” (479,924). Total = resolved + pending, so officer closures count as resolved. portal and dashboard, 22 Oct 2026
Why score trust 3/5 when you could not see status?
The public dashboard and the levels count. The post-filing screens are unverified. app test, 22 Oct 2026
Did you file a real complaint?
No. We stopped before Review & Submit. app test, 22 Oct 2026
What if all three levels fail?
RTI for the records, the Lokayukta for officer misconduct, or Sakala for late services. Sakala compensation figures: unverified. portal, 22 Oct 2026
